yuklanadi. Keyingisidan tezlashadi. Grafik karta boʻlmagan noutbuklarda
har bir gapirish bir necha soniya davom etadi — bu halol raqam, hech
narsa yashirilmagan.

=== ENGLISH ===

🖥 Kotiba now runs on Windows too!

Kotiba — hold a key, speak, and it writes what you said. Uzbek, Russian and
English, and the whole point: YOUR VOICE NEVER LEAVES YOUR LAPTOP.
Everything runs locally — no internet needed, nothing sent to a server.

📦 The file is large — about 1.2 GB. That's because all three language
models are bundled inside the installer, so nothing needs to download
after you install it.

⚠️ During install, Windows will show a blue "Windows protected your PC"
screen. This is expected — the app doesn't have a paid certificate, that's
all. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

⌨️ Hotkey: RIGHT CTRL. Hold it down while you speak, let go, and the text
appears wherever your cursor is, in any app. (Different from the Mac
version since Windows has no Command key — changeable in Settings.)

⏱ The first dictation in each language is a bit slow while its model
loads — after that it's quick. On a laptop with no graphics card, expect
a few seconds per dictation — that's an honest number, nothing hidden.
